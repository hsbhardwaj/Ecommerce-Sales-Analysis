# E-Commerce Sales Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce sales dataset to understand sales performance, profitability, customer segments, product performance, and regional trends.

The analysis was performed using Python and popular data analysis and visualization libraries.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Technology is the primary profit-driving category.
- Office Supplies provides strong and reliable profitability.
- Furniture generates high sales but has comparatively low profitability.
- Copiers, Phones, and Accessories are among the strongest-performing sub-categories.
- Tables and Bookcases are significant loss-making sub-categories.
- The West region leads in overall revenue and profitability.
- California is the top-performing state.
- Sales show a strong upward trend over the analyzed period.
- Sales are generally strongest during the fourth quarter, particularly November and December.
- Higher discount levels are frequently associated with lower or negative profit.

## 📈 Recommendations

1. Set discount limits for low-margin Furniture products.
2. Focus marketing efforts on high-margin Technology products.
3. Review consistently loss-making products before increasing their inventory or sales efforts.
4. Prepare inventory and marketing campaigns before the Q4 seasonal sales increase.
